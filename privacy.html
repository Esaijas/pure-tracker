Pure Tracker — Privacy

What the app does with your Google account, in plain terms.

Pure Tracker is a sports tracker that records activities on your own phone. It has no accounts, no servers and no analytics. The only thing it ever sends anywhere is what this page describes.

What it asks Google for

One permission, drive.file. It lets an app see and manage only the files it created itself. Pure Tracker is structurally unable to read the rest of your Google Drive — not as a matter of good behaviour, but because it was never granted the ability.

What it writes there

Your activity files, in a folder in your own Google Drive: the route, the times, the heights, heart rate where a sensor recorded one, and any photographs attached to an activity. Nothing else. It is a copy of what is already on your phone, so that losing the phone does not lose the history.

Where else it goes

Nowhere. Nothing is sent to the developer, to any analytics service, or to any third party. There is no account to create and no profile to build.

The files are not encrypted

Anyone who can get into that Google account can read them, including where you live. An activity file records where it started, and for most people that is their front door.

That is a deliberate choice rather than an oversight. Encrypting would stop the folder being openable by hand, which is the point of having it, and would add a lost-passphrase failure worse than losing the phone.

The list of who can read a backup is longer than it first looks: you, anyone who gets into your account, Google, anyone Google is legally compelled to answer, any other app that was ever granted full-Drive access, and every computer running Drive for desktop signed into that account.

Turning it off

Sign out inside the app, which revokes its access at Google rather than only forgetting it on the phone. You can also revoke it yourself at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The files already written stay in your Drive. Delete them there whenever you like; the app will not put them back unless you ask it to back up again.

What stays on the phone

Everything, whether or not you ever sign in. The backup is optional and the app records, draws and exports without it.
