Pure Tracker

A sports tracker that keeps your history on your own phone.

Pure Tracker records walks, runs, rides, swims, skiing and a few dozen other things, on Android. It has no account, no server and no analytics. What it measures stays on the phone unless you ask it to go somewhere.

What it does

  • Records a route, a pace, a climb and a heart rate, with the screen off.
  • Draws the history back — by activity, by week, by month, by year.
  • Exports any activity as a GPX file you can open anywhere.
  • Optionally copies your activities to a folder in your own Google Drive, so that losing the phone does not lose the history.

The Drive backup is optional

If you turn it on, the app asks Google for one narrow permission — drive.file — which lets it see only the files it wrote itself and nothing else in your Drive. What that means in practice, and who can read a backup once it exists, is set out on the privacy page.
